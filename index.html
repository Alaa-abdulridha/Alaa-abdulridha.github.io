Alaa Hermes Assistant

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Alaa Hermes Assistant is a personal AI secretary run by Alaa Abdulridha on a personal home server. It helps its owner manage their own Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Docs and Sheets: summarizing email, drafting replies for the owner's approval, and keeping the schedule organized.

It is not offered to the public and serves only its owner's own Google account.
