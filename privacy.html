Privacy Policy – Alaa Hermes Assistant

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Who uses this app

Alaa Hermes Assistant is a private, single-user app. Its only user is its owner, Alaa Abdulridha. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses and why

  • Gmail (read, modify, send): to summarize and organize the owner's email and to draft replies. Messages are sent only after the owner approves each one.
  • Google Calendar: to show the owner's schedule, find conflicts and add events the owner asks for.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: to find and read documents the owner asks about, and to create or update files at the owner's request.
  • Google Contacts (read only): to recognize people the owner corresponds with.

Where data is processed

Data is processed on the owner's own server at home. Content needed to answer the owner's requests may be sent to the AI model provider the owner uses (Anthropic) solely to produce the answer. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's server, readable only by the assistant's own system account, and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops all further access.
